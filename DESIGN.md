# INONDA LTD estimator design

Reference: https://scullion.ie/?ref=siteinspire, inspected in the browser on 2026-10-25. This is a requested style adaptation, not a pixel-for-pixel clone. The working estimator, supplied INONDA logo, company palette and company-only media remain the product constraints.

## Observed reference

The home reel alternates paired full-bleed architecture photography with large solid-colour panels and smaller inset photographs. Large, light grotesk typography, restrained navigation, generous negative space and a full-screen menu define the hierarchy. The source branding extraction identifies Founders Grotesk. Measurements of margins are visual estimates; no proprietary font or Scullion image is copied into the website.

## INONDA implementation

- Ocean Blue #0077B6, Dark Navy #023E8A and white.
- Helvetica Neue / Helvetica / Arial system font stack, with large regular-weight display headings, compact navigation and readable form text.
- Two equal desktop hero columns: company property media and a navy panel with headline, inset kitchen photograph and an estimate action.
- Original supplied logo, preserved proportions and colours, used in the header, footer and assistant launcher.
- Flat service panels, image-led selectable service cards and a navy live estimate. No fabricated reviews or customer claims.
- Mobile stacking, large form controls, a bottom estimate/assistant dock, visible inspection action and RTL layout for Hebrew.
- Greek default; English, Russian, Ukrainian, Hebrew and Romanian; language changes preserve calculation data.

## Pricing presentation

Every public monetary input and estimate includes 19% VAT. Commercial pricing remains applied internally while displayed rate inputs and formulas show the applicable price. No public copy, assistant explanation or exported plan identifies the internal commercial percentage. Rates remain illustrative and editable; site assessment and a written offer are required.
